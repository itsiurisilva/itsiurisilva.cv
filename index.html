About

I'm a Data Analyst who starts with the problem, not the dashboard: what's actually going wrong here, and what data would prove it? That habit of digging until the real issue surfaces — then building whatever's needed to fix it — comes from an engineering background (Arduino, PLCs at ETLA) as much as from Apps Script pipelines and Power BI models at Konecta.

If a process is manual, I ask why it still is. If the data to answer that doesn't exist yet, I build the pipeline that creates it.
