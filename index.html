My Journey

It all started with a school field trip to ABB in the Netherlands I fell in love with the logic behind the screen, experimenting with Flash to make things move the way I intent.

Age 12

Hardware DIY

My Mom enrolled me in a programming course for kids where I found my initial passion for hardware. I started connecting to an MSP430 microcontrollers from old Texas Instruments calculators to program my own mini-games.

Age 16

The Foundation: ETLA

Joined Escola Tecnológica do Litoral Alentejano. My interest scaled from microcontrollers to industrial automation, mastering Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and PLCs.

Age 18+

Started getting involved in the developers community (GitHub, Stack Overflow, Reddit, Hashnode, etc...). Driven by curiosity, I dedicated myself to learning new languages to solving community problems, mostly things I had no clue.
